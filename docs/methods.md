# Methods and evidence

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026

Afterhours Fitness is a planning tool. It combines published guidance with explicit product rules; the complete app program has not been tested as a clinical intervention.

## Strength training

The routine uses one squat, hinge, push, pull, and trunk movement selected from the equipment and limitations entered during setup. Regular sessions use 2–3 working sets. The default schedule is three nonconsecutive days, which can be changed.

The main reference is the 2026 American College of Sports Medicine position stand, an overview of 137 systematic reviews covering more than 30,000 healthy adults. It found that progressive resistance training improves strength and other measures of physical function. Strength outcomes were enhanced by complete range of motion, 2–3 sets, and at least two sessions per week.

- Currier BS, et al. *Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults: An Overview of Reviews.* Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise. 2026. [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843416/)
- US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. *Adult Activity: An Overview.* [CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html)

The app increases a load only after all saved working sets reach the top of the repetition range, technique is reported as controlled, and effort is no higher than 8 out of 10. This progression gate is a conservative product rule, not a claim taken verbatim from either source.

## Energy and protein estimates

Resting energy is estimated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. Total daily energy expenditure multiplies that result by the activity factor chosen by the user. The fat-loss setting starts at 90% of estimated maintenance and rounds to the nearest 50 kcal. These values are estimates and should be reviewed against actual trends.

- Mifflin MD, et al. *A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals.* American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. 1990. [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/)

The optional protein target is 1.6 g per kg of body weight. A 2018 meta-analysis found a breakpoint near 1.62 g/kg/day for additional fat-free-mass gains during resistance training in the populations it studied.

- Morton RW, et al. *A systematic review, meta-analysis and meta-regression of the effect of protein supplementation on resistance training-induced gains in muscle mass and strength in healthy adults.* British Journal of Sports Medicine. 2018. [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/)

## Food data and recipes

Food composition uses the closest supported regional source: FSANZ AFCD for Australia, DOST-FNRI for the Philippines, and Health Promotion Board references for Singapore. Open Food Facts supplies packaged products and barcode matches. Recipe totals are arithmetic estimates from edible weights.

- FSANZ. [Australian Food Composition Database](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/science-data/food-nutrient-databases/afcd/data-files)
- DOST-FNRI. [Philippine Food Composition Table Online](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library)
- Singapore Health Promotion Board. [Energy and Nutrient Composition of Food](https://focos.hpb.gov.sg/eservices/ENCF/)
- USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service. [Leftovers and food safety](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)

Starting prices are editable placeholders. They are not live market prices. Allergy filtering can only act on declared ingredients and cannot verify manufacturing cross-contact or product labels.

## Scheduling

Meal and workout timing is derived from the wake, sleep, work, preparation, and preferred workout times entered by the user. It does not attempt to predict a physiologically optimal time. Workouts are placed before work when the selected duration fits; otherwise the app checks the configured overnight backup window. Meal suggestions are spread through the waking period. All times remain editable.
